Knot to pad

One thread, a century long

Round nodes mark the hand-spun era. Square nodes mark the era of circuits and code. Watch the shape change as the story moves forward.

Image sources: Wikimedia Commons.

1918 – 1920s

Khadi & the Swadeshi movement

Hand-spun cotton becomes an act of economic protest — choosing homespun cloth over imported textile, thread by thread.

1950s – 60s

Steel towns rise

Bhilai, Rourkela and Durgapur come up as newly independent India builds its own heavy industry from the ground up.

1960s – 70s

The Green Revolution

High-yield seeds, irrigation and fertiliser push the country from food shortages toward food surplus.

1991

Economic liberalisation

Trade and licensing reforms open the door to a modern, competitive private sector — the hinge point of the story.

1990s – 2000s

The IT services boom

Indian software talent goes global, from Bengaluru campuses to back offices on every continent.

2009 – 2016

Aadhaar & the JAM trinity

Digital identity, bank accounts and mobile connections lay the rails for direct, leak-proof benefit transfer.

2016

UPI goes live

Instant, interoperable digital payments arrive at national scale — built at home, now studied abroad.

May 2020

Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan announced

Amid a global pandemic, self-reliance is named as an explicit economic strategy — with five pillars attached to it.

2020 – 21

PLI schemes roll out

Production-linked incentives target electronics, pharma, textiles and more — paying for value added at home.

2021 – 2023

Semiconductor Mission & Chandrayaan-3

India commits to building chips at home, and in August 2023 lands near the Moon's south pole — a first for any nation.

2024 onward

Fabs & gigafactories, under construction

Chip plants and green-energy gigafactories break ground. The next knot in the thread is still being tied.
