A handloom weaver, Kutch

"When a buyer overseas asks where the weave is from, I get to say Kutch. That sentence didn't used to travel this far."

A final-year engineering student, Pune

"We stopped asking 'which company will hire us' and started asking 'what can we build.' That's a different kind of ambition."

A wheat farmer, Punjab

"I check today's mandi price on my phone before I decide where to sell. That one habit changed who has the upper hand in the deal."

A startup founder, Bengaluru

"We built the product for a problem in our own city. Turns out three other countries had the same problem — so now we export it."

A retired army officer, Dehradun

"I served for thirty years watching equipment come from elsewhere. Seeing the same list turn domestic, one line at a time, means something."

A class 11 student, team of two

"We built this website in HTML, CSS and JavaScript for a school competition. Small thing — but it's ours, start to finish."
